About

We love coffee

Our vision is to build a café that celebrates slow mornings in a fast world: a place that inspires pause, presence, and the pleasure of a truly great cup.

Born from a
simple idea

We started with one goal: to bring the quality of world-class specialty coffee into a neighborhood setting, accessible, unpretentious, and genuinely excellent.

What began as curiosity about origin and process turned into a craft and a community. Today, Daily Grind is home to regulars who've been coming in since day one.

At the intersection of
origin and craft

Every bag we source is traceable to a single farm. We visit our partners, pay above fair-trade prices, and share the story of each harvest with you.
